Get your export in 30 seconds

  1. Open the chat and tap its name at the top.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat → Without Media.
  3. Tap Save to Files, then choose that .zip here.
Make it one tap with an iPhone Shortcut Get the Chat Wrapped Shortcut

Or build it yourself in a minute, in the Shortcuts app:

  1. Tap +, name it Chat Wrapped. In its settings, turn on Show in Share Sheet and receive Files.
  2. Add Save File: turn off Ask Where to Save, set the subpath to Chat Wrapped/ and turn on Overwrite.
  3. Add Open URLs with https://wrapped.jashparmar.com/?from=shortcut.

Now Export Chat → Chat Wrapped saves the chat and opens this page, ready to pick it. Websites can't read your Files on their own, which is exactly why this stays private.

  1. Tap Install app at the top of this page (or Chrome menu → Add to Home screen). One time only.
  2. In WhatsApp, open the chat → ⋮ → More → Export chat → Without media.
  3. Pick Chat Wrapped in the share menu. Your Wrapped starts straight away, on your phone.

Not installed? Save the .txt to your phone and choose it here instead.

  1. Open Telegram Desktop and the chat you want.
  2. Click ⋮ → Export chat history.
  3. Untick photos/videos and set format to JSON.
  4. Drop the result.json here.

Private by design

  • 🔒 Read on your device. Your chat is parsed by your browser. There is no upload and nothing is stored.
  • 🧱 Locked down. A strict Content Security Policy only lets this page talk to its own server, so nothing can quietly send your chat elsewhere.
  • 📊 Counted, not tracked. No cookies and no tracking scripts. The site counts visits and button taps (like "demo started") as anonymous daily totals, never anything from your chat.
  • 🤖 AI sees numbers. The optional verdict sends stats like "P1 replies in 3 min". Names are swapped back on your device.
